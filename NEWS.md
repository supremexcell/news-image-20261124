### 2. Jensen Huang is back at Nvidia; Wall Street roars
- Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president
  and director on Monday, 11 days after he resigned over affairs,
  including one with Alice Huang, an alleged Chinese-intelligence asset
  and Megaspeed executive. Trump says he pressed the board directly
  ("I made the deal"); VP Vance worked the board over the weekend; AG
  Todd Blanche told Trump on Saturday that Huang is not a DOJ target and
  there is no evidence he broke laws or is a national-security risk.
- Market reaction, Monday 23 Nov close, first reported 24 Nov: Nvidia
  +31.5% to $165.05, adding ~$952bn of market cap in one day — the
  largest one-day gain on record for a US company. S&P 500 +4.73% to
  7,237.17; Nasdaq +6.51% to 24,089.56; Dow +3.01% to 49,595.31 — the
  best day since April 2025. Megaspeed was placed on the US trade
  blacklist.
- Date note: the reinstatement was announced Monday 23 Nov (US morning);
  the inside reporting (CNN, WSJ, The Times) and the record market close
  were first reported 24 Nov, and the 23 Nov edition was built before the
  announcement. This is the first edition that can carry it.
- Sources: CNN, WSJ, Barron's, The Times, TradingKey, The Hindu
  BusinessLine.
